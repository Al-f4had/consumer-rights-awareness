A PRACTICAL CHECKLIST

Steps to raise a consumer grievance

Keep records and use the appropriate channel. The best route depends on the facts and type of dispute.

01

Contact the seller or service provider

Explain the issue clearly and request a reasonable solution. Use the company's official customer-care channel and note the complaint or ticket number.

02

Collect and preserve evidence

Keep the invoice, order ID, payment proof, warranty, product photographs, screenshots, emails and messages. Save copies and note important dates.

03

Approach the National Consumer Helpline

NCH is a pre-litigation grievance-facilitation channel. You can register a grievance online or through its phone and other supported channels. Keep the docket number.

Open National Consumer Helpline →
04

Consider filing before a Consumer Commission

If the dispute remains unresolved, you may consider filing a formal complaint before the appropriate Consumer Commission through the official e-Jagriti portal. Check current jurisdiction, documents, fees and limitation rules.
