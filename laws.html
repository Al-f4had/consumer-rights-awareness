Who does what?

Different institutions have different roles. Choosing the right channel matters.

National Consumer Helpline (NCH)

A pre-litigation grievance-facilitation channel that helps consumers raise grievances and seek resolution through its network and partner organisations.

IMPORTANT TERMS

Understand the language

Defect

A fault or imperfection in the quality, quantity, potency, purity or standard of goods, as defined by the Act.

Deficiency

A fault, imperfection, shortcoming or inadequacy in the quality or manner of performance of a service.

Unfair trade practice

A trade practice that falls within the Act's definition, involving specified unfair or deceptive methods to promote the sale or supply of goods or services.

Misleading advertisement

An advertisement that falls within the statutory definition, for example by falsely describing a product or making a misleading representation.

Product liability

Legal responsibility, in circumstances defined by the Act, for harm caused by a defective product or related service.

Redressal

A remedy that may be ordered by a Consumer Commission, depending on the facts and applicable law.
