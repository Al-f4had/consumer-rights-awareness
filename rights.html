CONSUMER RIGHTS

Know your rights. Use them wisely.

The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 recognises six fundamental consumer rights. Explore what each one means in everyday life.

Home/Know Your Rights

The six consumer rights

Search by right, keyword or example.

01
🛡️

Right to Safety

Protection against hazardous goods and services that may affect life and property.

Example: A consumer should be protected from an unsafe electrical appliance.

02
ⓘ

Right to be Informed

Access to accurate information about quality, quantity, price, ingredients and standards.

Example: Check product labels, MRP, expiry date and mandatory declarations.

03
⇄

Right to Choose

Access to a variety of goods and services at competitive prices, without unfair restrictions.

Example: A buyer should not be unfairly forced to purchase an unwanted add-on.

04
◉

Right to be Heard

Consumer interests and complaints should receive due consideration at appropriate forums.

Example: A consumer can raise a grievance through an appropriate complaint channel.

05
⚖

Right to Seek Redressal

Seek appropriate remedies against unfair trade practices, defects and deficiencies in services.

Example: Depending on the case, a Commission may order repair, replacement, refund or compensation.

06
▤

Right to Consumer Education

Learn about consumer rights and the knowledge needed to make informed decisions.

Example: Understand complaint procedures before making an important purchase.
