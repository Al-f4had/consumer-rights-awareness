Six basic consumer rights

Search for a right:

1. Right to Safety

Protection from dangerous goods and services.

2. Right to be Informed

Correct information about quality, quantity, price and product details.

3. Right to Choose

Freedom to choose from different goods and services at fair prices.

4. Right to be Heard

The consumer's complaint and interests should receive attention.

5. Right to Seek Redressal

Consumers can seek a suitable solution for eligible complaints.

6. Right to Consumer Education

Consumers should learn about their rights and responsibilities.

Remember

Always check the price, expiry date and details before buying a product.
