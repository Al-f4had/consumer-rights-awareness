EVERYDAY SITUATIONS

Problems consumers commonly face

Knowing the warning signs is the first step towards protecting yourself.

▧

Defective products

A product does not work as promised, is damaged or fails to meet required standards.

◉

Misleading advertisements

Promotional claims create a false impression or hide important conditions.

⌁

Online shopping issues

An order is not delivered, a refund is withheld or the item differs from its description.

₹

Overcharging

A seller charges more than the displayed or legally applicable price.
